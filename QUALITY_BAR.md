# Quality bar

- Truth: no invented users, posts, news, answers, activity counts, or success states.
- Completion: every visible submission or administration action is backed by a database route and a tested permission boundary.
- First viewport: one legible statement, one primary action, and six real destinations; no invented activity.
- Motion: finite signal introduction and bounded, pausable particles; reduced-motion remains complete.
- Accessibility: keyboard-visible focus, semantic labels, horizontal mobile navigation, no color-only states, and 320px minimum support.
- Privacy: collect only required account fields, avoid request logging, store hashed passwords/session tokens, expire sessions absolutely after 60 days, and purge administrator application details after decision retention.
- Performance: self-host visual assets, serve immutable build assets, and keep the first route free of third-party trackers.
