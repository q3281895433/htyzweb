# Direction contract — 夜自习后的信号窗

FORM seed key: `assigned-03-night-study-signal-window`

## World

The interface behaves like a school building after evening study: a near-black exterior, warm paper-lit windows, and one yellow-green signal line that links rooms without turning the site into a dashboard. Content appears as editorial sheets and horizontal channels rather than same-size cards.

## First viewport

The homepage opens as one full-height signal field. The product name and purpose occupy the left side as a large, quiet statement. On the right, a glass signal console presents six real destinations: 空间动态、校园墙、投稿、校园新闻、问答、建议. The console contains real counts or honest empty states only; it never displays invented activity.

## Visitor path

Visitors move from the immersive introduction into a real latest-content strip, then community rules, privacy commitments, and a single clear route to register or contribute. Returning users reach their tasks in one navigation action.

## Signature interaction

The console responds to hover and keyboard focus. Bounded particles and lines respond to pointer movement and clicks; users can turn them off. It persists no behavioral profile. Touch, keyboard, and reduced-motion users receive the same destinations without relying on motion.

## Cross-surface reach

Forms, lists, post-management tools, news, and Q&A reuse the window/paper relationship: dark navigation field, warm reading surface, thin signal rules, tabular status labels, and state conveyed by words and marks in addition to color.

## Motion grammar

One coordinated signal sweep introduces the first viewport. Lightweight particle motion is bounded, pauses when hidden, and can be disabled manually; other motion is interaction feedback. Content remains visible before animation, and `prefers-reduced-motion` removes nonessential movement.

## Honest risk

The dark evening world could reduce reading comfort if carried everywhere. Long-form content therefore moves onto warm paper surfaces with high-contrast ink, while dark treatment stays with navigation and orientation.

## Raised quality lines

- From the daylight-section challenger: make time and movement legible through precise annotations, not decorative glow.
- From the film-rail challenger: every state is a written mark—已发布、已删除、已封禁—not color alone.
- From the ink-basin challenger: interaction must be a real browser behavior, not a static background effect.
